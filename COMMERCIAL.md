# Commercial licensing

cryosweep is released under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
That licence covers **noncommercial use only**. This page explains who is already
covered, who is not, and how to get a licence if you are not.

## You are already covered — no licence, no cost, no need to contact anyone

The licence grants use for a *permitted purpose*, and it defines that broadly for
research and education. Quoting the licence itself:

> Use by any charitable organization, educational institution, public research
> organization, public safety or health organization, environmental protection
> organization, or government institution is use for a permitted purpose
> **regardless of the source of funding or obligations resulting from the funding**.

In practice that means the following need **no** commercial licence:

- universities and university groups, including students and staff
- public research institutes and national laboratories
- government and public-sector laboratories
- charitable and nonprofit research organizations
- **industry-funded work at any of the above** — the funding source does not matter
- personal use, study, and evaluation by an individual

You may also use, modify and redistribute it for those purposes, and you may
publish results obtained with it. If you do, a citation is appreciated — see
[CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).

## You need a commercial licence

If the use is for or on behalf of a **company or other commercial organization** —
for example measurement services sold to customers, industrial R&D inside a
for-profit business, or bundling cryosweep into a commercial product or instrument.

## How to get one

Email **info@cryosweep.org** with a short description of:

1. your organization,
2. how you intend to use cryosweep (internal analysis, a service you sell, embedding
   it in a product), and
3. roughly how many people or instruments are involved.

Terms are agreed case by case. There is no public price list yet; a short email is
genuinely the fastest route, and an early conversation is welcome even if you are
only evaluating.

## Uncertain which side you are on?

Ask. A one-line email is cheaper for both of us than a wrong assumption, and
questions about scope are never treated as an admission of anything.

## Other arrangements

Also by email: instrument-vendor and reseller partnerships, OEM/embedding terms,
support or development contracts, and alternative licensing for a specific
collaboration.
