# Code of conduct

This is a small scientific software project. The expectations are the ordinary
ones of professional scientific collaboration:

- Be respectful. Criticize code, data, and methods — not people.
- Disagreements are settled by evidence: a file that reproduces the problem, a
  measurement, a test. That is also how this codebase itself settles them.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks, in issues, pull
  requests, or any other project space.
- Assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors and with people
  reporting problems in imperfect English.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour go to **info@cryosweep.org** and will be
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove content and
may block contributors who do not follow these expectations.
