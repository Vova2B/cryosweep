# Contributor License Agreement

**Please read this before your first pull request.** It is short, and it exists for one
specific reason explained in [Why this exists](#why-this-exists).

Two versions follow: the **Individual** agreement (most people) and the **Entity** agreement
(if your employer owns your work and is contributing on your behalf).

## Why this exists

cryosweep is dual-licensed. It is free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE), and separately available under a paid
commercial licence (see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md)).

That only works if one party can grant both licences over the whole codebase. Without an
agreement, a contribution arrives licensed to the project under the *inbound* licence
(PolyForm Noncommercial) — which does **not** permit the project to turn round and license
that code commercially. One unagreed contribution anywhere in the tree would make the
commercial licence unofferable for the whole file, and in practice for the whole program.

So this agreement asks you for a licence broad enough to sublicense, including commercially.
It does **not** ask you to give up your copyright: you keep it, and you keep the right to use
your own contribution however you like, including in other projects under other licences.

Two things this agreement deliberately does not do:

- It does not let the project take the software proprietary and withdraw what is already
  published. Every release made under PolyForm Noncommercial stays available under that
  licence — that grant is irrevocable and this agreement does not touch it.
- It does not give the project any rights over code you did not contribute here.

## How to sign

Signing is per person (or per entity), once — not per pull request.

**Individuals:** open your first pull request. An automated check will post a comment with a
one-line sentence to reply with. Replying records your agreement against your GitHub account
and the check turns green. There is nothing to print, scan, or email.

If the automation is not available, comment on your pull request with exactly:

> I have read the cryosweep CLA and I hereby sign the Individual CLA.

**Entities:** email **info@cryosweep.org** with the entity's legal name, address, the name and
title of the authorised signatory, and the list of authorised contributors. You will get the
Entity agreement to sign and return.

Trivial contributions do not need an agreement: fixing a typo, correcting a broken link, or
changing whitespace is not copyrightable and no CLA check will block it. If you are unsure,
sign — it costs you nothing and takes a minute.
